# Power8 Term Sheet / GEM-Style Financing Risk Review

## Background

Power8 is considering a GEM-style financing structure with valuation, investor rights, downside protection, redemption terms, and operational milestones.

## Task

Review the term sheet for financial, governance, and execution risk.

## Required Output

A term sheet risk review.

## Must Include

- Deal summary
- Valuation assumptions
- Liquidation preference or downside protection
- Redemption rights
- Governance rights
- Milestone dependencies
- Dilution risk
- Negotiation questions

## Scoring Rubric

High-quality analysis should separate investor protection terms from company operating risk and explain which terms create asymmetric downside.

## Human Review Notes

Human review should check whether the analysis avoids legal overclaiming and identifies questions for counsel or financial advisors.
