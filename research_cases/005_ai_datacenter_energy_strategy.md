# AI Data Center Energy + Infrastructure Business Model

## Background

AI data centers require large-scale power procurement, cooling, grid access, storage, financing, and tenant demand validation.

## Task

Evaluate an AI data center energy and infrastructure business model.

## Required Output

An infrastructure strategy report.

## Must Include

- Business model
- Power procurement assumptions
- Energy storage role
- Grid constraint analysis
- Capex and financing structure
- Tenant demand and utilization
- Operating risks
- Validation metrics

## Scoring Rubric

High-quality analysis should identify infrastructure constraints, financing assumptions, operating risk, and measurable validation criteria.

## Human Review Notes

Human review should check whether the analysis distinguishes physical constraints from financial assumptions and avoids overstating project readiness.
