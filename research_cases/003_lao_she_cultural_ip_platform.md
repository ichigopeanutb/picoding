# Lao She Cultural IP Platform Strategy

## Background

A cultural IP platform could connect Lao She's literary heritage with digital experiences, education, licensing, content products, and sustainable commercialization.

## Task

Design a strategy that respects cultural integrity while exploring scalable product and business models.

## Required Output

A strategy memo.

## Must Include

- Strategic thesis
- Audience segments
- Product and content lines
- Education and licensing opportunities
- Cultural integrity risks
- Commercial model
- Validation plan

## Scoring Rubric

High-quality analysis should balance cultural respect, audience development, product strategy, and revenue model validation.

## Human Review Notes

Human review should check cultural sensitivity, rights assumptions, and whether the commercialization logic is realistic.
