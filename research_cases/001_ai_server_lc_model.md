# AI Server + LC + Listed Company Financing Model

## Background

A proposed AI server leasing model uses LC financing, listed company equity participation, IDC deployment, tenant lease revenue, and possible share price upside incentives.

## Task

Assess whether the business model is financially coherent, operationally plausible, and governance-safe.

## Required Output

A structured risk report.

## Must Include

- Business logic
- Cash flow structure
- LC repayment path
- Tenant default risk
- Server utilization assumptions
- IDC deployment assumptions
- Related-party transaction risk
- Share price incentive risk

## Scoring Rubric

High-quality analysis should identify financing risk, operational risk, governance risk, cash flow timing risk, and missing assumptions.

## Human Review Notes

Human review should confirm whether the report understands the financing mechanics and avoids treating speculative upside as guaranteed cash flow.
