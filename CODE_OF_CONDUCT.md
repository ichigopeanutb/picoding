# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in PiCoding welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone.

PiCoding brings together people from software, research, engineering, AI, and physical-system domains. We value clear communication, technical curiosity, good-faith disagreement, and care for contributors who are learning.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent while still asking for clarity.
- Give feedback that is specific, actionable, and kind.
- Explain technical assumptions, especially when discussing physical models.
- Credit ideas, references, and prior work where appropriate.
- Help maintain a review culture that improves the work without discouraging contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Dismissive comments about a person's background, identity, or level of experience.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or maintainer processes.
- Misrepresenting the validation, safety, adoption, or maturity of project artifacts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove comments, close issues, request changes, or limit participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Enforcement should be fair, proportionate, and focused on protecting a healthy open-source environment.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers through the repository's preferred contact method. If the issue is sensitive, avoid posting private details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed with respect for privacy and context.
